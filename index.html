New Earth Journal — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 10 November 2026 · Last updated: 10 November 2026

The short version: Your journal belongs to you. New Earth Journal stores your entries and attached media locally on your device. The developer does not collect your journal content, require an account, run advertising trackers, or operate a server that receives your journal data.

1. About this policy

This policy explains how New Earth Journal (the “App”), published by ETenergy, handles information when you use the iPhone app. It applies to the App itself, not to information you separately choose to share through other services.

2. Information kept on your device

Depending on what you choose to record, the App may store the following locally on your iPhone:

  • Journal writing, dates, entry titles, reflections and preferences.
  • Photos, videos and voice recordings you add to your entries.
  • Moon memories and optional place or location information associated with memories.
  • Information needed to organise and restore your entries and attachments.

This information is stored for your use within the App. The developer does not receive or read it through an ETenergy account or server. The App does not require you to create a user account.

3. Data collection, analytics and advertising

ETenergy does not collect journal entries, personal information or usage analytics from the App, and the App does not use advertising or cross-app tracking. It does not include a developer-operated cloud synchronisation service.

Apple may independently process app distribution, crash reports or device diagnostics according to your Apple settings and its own privacy practices. See Apple's Privacy Policy.

4. Permissions you control

The App may request access when you use a feature that needs it:

  • Photos and videos: to choose existing media or save content you select for your journal.
  • Camera and microphone: to capture photos, videos or voice notes when you choose.
  • Location: to attach an optional place to a journal entry. You may use the App without allowing location access.

When you ask the App to resolve coordinates into a place name, Apple's geocoding service may process the location request. This is a service provided by Apple, not a collection of journal content by ETenergy. You can also enter or change a place manually. Device permissions can be changed in iOS Settings.

5. Exports, backups and sharing

The App offers an export feature so you can create a portable copy of your journal and its media. Journal TAR/PAX exports are not encrypted by the App. Keep exported files in a private, secure location. If you save, send or upload an export using Files, iCloud Drive, email or another service, that service's privacy and security terms apply. ETenergy does not automatically receive those exports.

System-level iPhone backup features, if enabled by you, are controlled through your Apple account and device settings rather than an ETenergy account.

6. Data retention and deletion

Your journal information remains on your device until you delete it or remove the App and its local data. Any copies you exported or backed up elsewhere are separate and must be managed or deleted from those locations yourself. ETenergy cannot retrieve, recover or erase files stored only on your device.

7. Children's privacy

The App is a personal journaling tool. ETenergy does not knowingly collect personal data from children or other users through the App.

8. Changes

We may update this policy when the App changes. The updated version and its date will be published at this address. If new features introduce different data practices, we will update the relevant disclosures.

9. Contact

For privacy questions or support, email elmartirza@gmail.com. Please do not email private journal entries, personal recordings, or unencrypted journal archives unless you intentionally choose to share them.

For common questions see the New Earth Journal Support page.
