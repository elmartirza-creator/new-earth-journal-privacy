Help with your private journal on iPhone.

Contact support

Email elmartirza@gmail.com with the subject New Earth Journal Support.

If reporting an issue, describe the screen, what happened, your iPhone model, and iOS version. Please do not send private entries, recordings, photographs or full journal archives unless you intentionally wish to share them.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account?
No. Your journal is designed for private use on your device.

Where are entries saved?
Entries and attachments are stored locally in the App's device storage. Keep a separate export in a safe place if you want a portable backup.

Can I add photographs, videos and voice recordings?
Yes. You can attach media to journal entries and review it later.

Does the App need my location?
No. Location memories are optional. You can choose to use location services or enter a place manually.

How do I back up my journal?
Use the App's Export Journal option to create a portable archive and save it securely through the iOS share or Files interface. Exports are not encrypted by the App, so protect the copy yourself.

Can support access or restore my private journal?
No. ETenergy does not maintain a cloud copy of your entries and cannot retrieve them from our servers.
